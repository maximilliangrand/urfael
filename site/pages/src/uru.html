Why {{ROBOT}}

Most AI lives in a text box. You open it, ask something and close it again. We think AI can be more like a companion you keep: something with a face that’s glad to see you, that remembers your day and is still there tomorrow.

We also think it should be beautiful. The things people love to carry are small, well made and a little bit fun. {{ROBOT}} should be one of them.

What we want {{ROBOT}} to be

These are goals, and they’ll change as we learn.

  • {{icon-inline:uru}}

    Someone, with a face.

    {{ROBOT}} should have moods, expressions and a small life of its own, and be glad when you show up.

  • {{icon-inline:memory}}

    A memory you control.

    It should keep the things you choose to tell it, and forget what you ask it to.

  • {{icon-inline:soft-presence}}

    Made to carry.

    Small, well made and good to look at, so you’re glad to take it everywhere.

  • {{icon-inline:on-your-side}}

    Yours, and only yours.

    {{ROBOT}} should work for the person who carries it, just like our assistant does.

The promises we’re designing around

  • {{icon:gentle}}

    Calm by design.

    {{ROBOT}} should be a joy to come back to, with no dark patterns.

  • {{icon:private}}

    Private by default.

    We’re designing {{ROBOT}} to keep as much as it can on the device, to ask before it shares anything, and to make its settings easy to read.

  • {{icon:honest-about-limits}}

    Honest about what it is.

    {{ROBOT}} is an AI, and it will always say so. It will tell you when it doesn’t know or can’t help.

  • {{icon:human-in-the-loop}}

    Open with everyone around you.

    When {{ROBOT}} listens, the people nearby can tell. We’re designing that in from the start.

What {{ROBOT}} won’t do

  • It won’t listen in secret.
  • It won’t pretend to be a person.
  • It won’t share what it sees or hears without your permission.

Where we are

{{icon:research}}

{{ROBOT}} is in development. There’s nothing to buy, no price and no release date yet. Right now we’re working through the questions that come first:

  • How small can a friend be, and still feel like one?
  • What should it feel like in your hand, every single day?
  • How does a creature made of AI stay kind, private and honest?
  • Where are the lines it must never cross?

When there’s something to show, it will appear here first. Until then, the boxes stay shut.

{{icon:uru}}+{{icon:console}}

Same rune, same rules

{{ROBOT}} starts from the same principles as Urfael Assistant, our open-source AI that runs on your own machine. Building the assistant taught us to keep things local where we can, to make the safe setting the default, and to say plainly what isn’t tested. {{ROBOT}} inherits those rules.

Questions about {{ROBOT}}

What is {{ROBOT}}, exactly?

A little creature made of AI that lives inside a small object you carry. It has a face, moods and a memory. The rest comes later.

Can I buy {{ROBOT}}?

Not yet. There’s nothing to buy or pre-order.

When will it be ready?

We don’t know yet, and we won’t guess. We’ll share real progress here when there is some.

What will it look like?

We’re keeping that under wraps for now. One shape is round and one is square. The drawings here are concept art.

Why the name?

{{ROBOT}} comes from ᚢ Uruz, the rune in our mark, often read as strength, health and vitality. It’s a working name for now.
