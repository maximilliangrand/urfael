Why ᚢ

Our mark is ᚢ, Uruz, a rune from the Elder Futhark. It is often read as strength, health and vitality. We took it as a brief: make AI that leaves people a little stronger and a little better looked after.

We started with software, because that’s where trust is earned. The assistant lives on your machine, works for you, starts in its safest setting, and says plainly what hasn’t been tested yet.

{{ROBOT}} carries the same rules into a body, for the place where help matters most: home.

What we hold to

  • {{icon:on-your-side}}

    On your side.

    Our AI works for the person it lives with. The assistant runs on your hardware, under your settings.

  • {{icon:private}}

    Locked down by default.

    The safest setting comes first. Your archive and memory stay on your machine, and your messages go to the model you choose. You decide what to open up, and we tell you what each choice means.

  • {{icon:honest-about-limits}}

    Honest about limits.

    We publish what’s lightly tested right next to what works. If we don’t know yet, we say so.

  • {{icon:gentle}}

    Gentle by design.

    Soft words, soft shapes, no pressure. Help should feel like a kind neighbor, not a sales pitch.

  • {{icon:open-to-inspection}}

    Open to inspection.

    The assistant is open source under the MIT license. Anyone can read the code, run the tests and tell us where we’re wrong.

{{icon:tests}}
